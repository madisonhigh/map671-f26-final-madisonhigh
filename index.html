Potential Threats to Blackside Dace Habitat in Whitley County, Kentucky

Madison High for University of Kentucky Geography

Data from this project was downloaded from the KyGovMaps Open Data Portal. Data used for this project includes Blackside Dace Streams in Kentucky (2009), Incorporated City Boundaries in Kentucky (Polygons), Existing Industry in Kentucky, Permitted Mine Boundaries in Kentucky, State-Maintained Roads in Kentucky, and Kentucky County Polygons. Data accessed October, 2026.

Project design

  • All data clipped to the extent of Whitley County, Kentucky
  • Coordinate system used: EPSG:3089
  • Map scale: 1:250000
  • Map made on QGIS 3.44.14

Blackside dace are small minnows with a red underbelly and a black stripe down their sides. These fish are endemic to two river basins across Kentucky, Tennessee, and Virginia. According to the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, blackside dace are considered threatened under the 1987 U.S. Endangered Species Act. Since their habitat is constrained to such a narrow area, any potential threats to their habitat may be devastating to their population.
